**Slides.** Author the hour in **Quarto Reveal.js**. Treat PowerPoint as an export and podium fail-safe, not the master. Details: [`slides/README.md`](../slides/README.md). Do not dump this packet into 60 slides.

Print the **packet** (survey + workshops + ledger) at 11–12 pt, 1.15 line spacing, 1-inch margins. Combined length is roughly 30–40 pages; the survey alone is a bit under that and is meant to be read with the workshop recipes.
