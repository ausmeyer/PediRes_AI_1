# SYNTHETIC TEACHING CASE — NOT A REAL PATIENT

A 26-day-old infant born at 39 weeks is brought for a rectal temperature of 38.5 °C measured at home. The infant is well appearing on examination, has no focal bacterial infection, no clinical bronchiolitis, no vesicles, and had an uncomplicated nursery course. Blood and urine cultures have not yet been obtained. Urinalysis, procalcitonin, C-reactive protein, and absolute neutrophil count are not provided.

Teaching question: What does the cited AAP guideline say about lumbar puncture for an infant in this age group, and what missing information prevents a complete source-grounded answer?
